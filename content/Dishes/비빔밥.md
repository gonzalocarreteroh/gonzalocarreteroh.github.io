## Ingredients
비빔밥 is a very versatile dish so you may add more or add less ingredients.

##### Main
- Rice
- Carrots
- Spinach
- Bean sprouts
- Shiitake mushrooms
- Zucchini
- Minced beef
- Eggs

##### Sauces
- Sesame oil
- 고추장

##### Spices
- Garlic

## Instructions

In a frying pan, add a bit of sesame oil and cook (medium heat) each of the main ingredients (besides rice and eggs) separately, one after another. Set them aside as you cook them.

Assemble all the cooked ingredients on top of rice and add some 고추장, plus a fried egg. Mix well before eating.

You may make the meat or sauce preparation more complex for enhanced flavor following any of the references.
