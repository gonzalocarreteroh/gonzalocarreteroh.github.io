It is a variation of the dish 炸醬麵 (_zhájiàngmiàn_), brought to Korea by Chinese immigrants.

## Ingredients

##### Main
- Noodles
- Zucchini
- Onion
- Minced pork
##### Sauces
- Black bean paste
- Soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Cornstarch mix
##### Spices
- Ginger

## Instructions

In a frying pan, add the minced pork with the minced ginger into medium heat. When it is a bit cooked, add the soy and oyster sauces. Then the chopped zucchini and onions, and the black bean paste. Mix well. Add some water and the cornstarch mix to thicken the sauce.

Assemble the cooked noodles with the content of the frying pan directly into the serving bowl.
