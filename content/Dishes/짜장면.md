It is a variation of the dish 炸醬麵 (_zhájiàngmiàn_), brought to Korea by Chinese immigrants.

## Ingredients

##### Main
- Noodles (1 kg)
- Zucchini (3 large)
- Onion (3 large)
- Pork (4 steaks size)
##### Sauces
- Black bean paste (1 bag)
- Soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Cornstarch mix
##### Spices
- Ginger (1 medium)

## Instructions

Start cutting the zucchini and onions, into small squares but big enough to notice them in a bite. Cut the pork in cubes and the mince the ginger in really small pieces.

In a frying pan, add the pork with the ginger into medium heat. When it is a bit cooked, add the soy and oyster sauces. Then the zucchini and onions, and the black bean paste. Mix well. Add some water (no need to cover all, perhaps around half of the pot) and the cornstarch mix to thicken the sauce.

Assemble the cooked noodles with the content of the frying pan directly into the serving bowl.
